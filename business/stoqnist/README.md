# Stoqnist — Commercial Business and Project Participation

Status: **concept and research brief** (2026-11-13). Not a legal opinion, approved investment offer, investor solicitation, financial forecast, or incorporation decision.

## Purpose
Develop businesses and projects under the Stoqnist brand, generate income from their products, services, intellectual property and other commercial assets, and evaluate whether a small group of personally known investors can participate economically in selected projects.

The inspiration is historical Dutch trade and project participation, not a commitment to reproduce any historic corporate or securities structure.

## Founder decisions and working assumptions
- **Investors:** initially a small group of people personally known to the founder; residency, investor classification and exact number remain unknown.
- **Control:** the founder intends to retain control of the Stoqnist brand and central business even if external investors contribute capital.
- **Assets:** businesses and projects developed under the Stoqnist brand; specific assets, ownership chain, IP rights and valuations remain unverified.
- **Participation:** investments and returns should be linked to individual projects, with project-specific budgets, expenses, revenue, reporting and contractual economics.
- **Financial target:** deliberately deferred; no annual income, capital-raising or return target has been adopted.
- **Jurisdiction:** Netherlands is the starting point for company and regulatory research; investor and activity jurisdictions must be confirmed.

These are design inputs, **not** a determination that project participation can legally be offered or that a particular structure is appropriate.

## Commercialisation options to evaluate
Sale, licensing, subscription, service delivery and other lawful monetisation of project assets. Compare a simple operating business funded by the founder with project-level investment alternatives before adding legal entities.

## Legal structures to research, not adopt by default
Dutch BV holding/operating company, project-specific SPV, direct equity, STAK certificates, loans, contractual profit participation, cooperative and any relevant collective-investment arrangement. Compare founder control, investor rights, ring-fencing, governance, costs, accounting, taxation, transferability and wind-down.

**Critical gate:** A small, private, invitation-only group and separate project entities do not automatically exempt an arrangement from financial regulation. Before approaching investors or receiving money, obtain qualified Dutch legal analysis of the specific instrument and activities, including Wft, AIFMD, securities/prospectus rules, MiFID II and crowdfunding/ECSPR where applicable. Confirm tax and accounting treatment with qualified advisers.

## Work plan
1. Inventory prospective projects and clarify which legal person owns each asset/IP right.
2. Describe one pilot project's business model, budget, downside, revenue and proposed investor economics.
3. Have the Legal & Regulatory Research Specialist identify classifications, evidence gaps and professional-review questions.
4. Have the Venture & Capital Architect compare a no-investor baseline against the simplest viable project-participation structures.
5. Obtain Dutch legal/tax/accounting validation before any solicitation, offer, signed participation or receipt of funds.
6. Only after approval, implement governance, contracts, accounting, reporting and investor communications.

## Open questions
Investor residency and eligibility; whether returns derive from profits, revenue, interest or sale proceeds; minimum and maximum commitments; investor voting/information/exit rights; loss allocation; founder contributions; project/IP ownership; project closure and disputes; tax residency; actual budget.
